The Delphi method is a popular research tool. It relies on experts in a field reaching agreement on important issues. The Delphi method is a forecasting process that involves finding a consensus through anonymous questionnaire responses from experts. After each round of questionnaires, experts receive a summary of the last round. This allows them to adjust their answers based on the group response. Analogous estimating, also known as top-down estimating, uses the estimator's experience or historical data from past projects. It applies observed costs, duration, or resource needs to a current project or its parts. This technique is useful for producing estimates when not much information is available. A range estimate includes various possible values instead of just one number. It is often paired with a most likely estimate. A common type of range estimate is three-point estimation. I.INTRODUCTION

In software development, cost estimation is a crucial and challenging part of project planning. This project offers a working model for estimating the cost of implementing a new Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system. It combines estimation with expert judgment. Instead of using just one estimation method, this approach combines from multiple data sources to create a more trustworthy result.The methodology begins by gathering independent assessments from five senior team members with different technical backgrounds, including back-end developers, front-end developers, full-stack engineers, and experienced project managers. These expert opinions are compared with historical data from a previous inventory management system. Adjustments are made for differences in project scope complexity, integration needs, team experience, and technology maturity. By applying weighted averages to both estimation methods, with 60% for Delphi and 40% for Analog, and adding risk buffers for known and unknown uncertainties, the framework produces a cost estimate that reflects both the technical aspects of the project and the unpredictability of software development.
